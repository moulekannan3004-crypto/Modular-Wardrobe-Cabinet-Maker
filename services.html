Automated Factory Fabrication

Inside Our Factory: The 6-Stage Manufacturing Lifecycle

How every custom wardrobe panel and cabinetry carcass is manufactured with computerized precision before arriving at your home.

01

3D Laser Survey & CAD Modeling

Sub-millimeter laser audit of room angles, wall plumb, and electrical outlets. Blueprints are directly translated into 3D parametric CAD fabrication files.

02

Automated Nesting & Optimization

Computerized nesting algorithms arrange cutting patterns across raw timber boards to maximize grain alignment continuity and eliminate raw material waste.

03

5-Axis CNC Precision Sizing & Boring

German Homag CNC machinery routes panels to 0.1mm tolerance and precision-bores all 32mm system minifix, dowel, and shelf pin holes simultaneously.

04

Zero-Joint PUR Hot-Melt Edge Banding

2mm high-impact PVC/ABS edge tapes applied with polyurethane (PUR) adhesive at 200°C, producing an invisible joint that provides a 100% waterproof seal against humidity.

05

Finishing & Factory Optical QC

Clean-room PU lacquer application and scratch-resistant acrylic pressing. Every module undergoes strict optical tolerance inspection before barcoded honeycomb crating.

06

48h Dust-Free On-Site Assembly

Certified master fitters assemble pre-finished modular components with minifix fasteners in 1-2 days. Zero noisy cutting, zero sawdust, and zero toxic varnish odors in your home.

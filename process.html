Homeowner Preparation

What to Prepare for Your Measurement Visit

1. Clothing Count Estimate

Roughly count how many long dresses, suits, pairs of shoes, and watches you intend to store.

2. Note Power Outlets

Identify wall electrical outlets for connecting LED sensor transformers or built-in vanity mirrors.

3. Preferred Material Swatches

Review our website swatches (Oak, Matte PU, Fluted Glass) so our designer brings relevant samples.

Who We Are

Pioneering Modular Joinery & Cabinet Engineering

The Company: Founded in 2011 by master joinery engineer Henrik Vance, MODULARIS is an industry-leading architectural manufacturer specializing in bespoke modular wardrobes and premium custom cabinetry. What began as an artisan workshop in Munich has scaled into a state-of-the-art 40,000 sq. ft. automated manufacturing facility dedicated to eliminating the delays, noise, and airborne sawdust of traditional on-site carpentry.

Our Work

We design and fabricate custom modular wardrobes (hinged, sliding, walk-in, L-shaped alcoves), waterproof modular kitchens, floating media consoles, and luxury vanities.

Our Experience

15+ years of engineering mastery combining European craftsmanship with 5-axis German Homag CNC machinery, 0.1mm tolerances, and zero-joint PUR edge-banding.

Our Manufacturing & Material Standards

Industrial joinery specifications engineered for tropical humidity, structural stability, and lifetime reliability.

SPEC 01 • MOISTURE CORE

18mm HDHMR & BWP Cores

High-Density High Moisture Resistance boards (850+ kg/m³ density) and Boiling Water Proof Marine ply for wet zones with zero swelling.

  • 18mm HDHMR carcass walls & shelves
  • BWP Marine grade sink carcasses
  • 8mm rebated anti-racking back panel
Density: 850+ kg/m³ CARB 2 / E0 Grade
SPEC 02 • ZERO-JOINT PUR

PUR Hot-Melt Edge Banding

Polyurethane reactive hot-melt adhesive bonds 1.3mm–2mm PVC/ABS bands creating an imperceptible, moisture-impermeable seal on all edges.

  • Invisible zero glue line technology
  • 150°C heat & steam moisture resistance
  • Corner-rounding profile finishing
Adhesion: 100% Water-Proof DIN 68861
SPEC 03 • 200K CYCLES

Blum & Hettich Hardware

Austrian and German soft-close clip hinges and full-extension undermount slides certified for 200,000 opening cycles without sagging.

  • Integrated BLUMOTION fluid soft-close
  • Tandembox 40kg–70kg drawer capacity
  • Concealed 3-way eccentric cam adjustment
Cycle Life: 200,000+ Lifetime Tested
SPEC 04 • 0.1MM CNC

5-Axis CNC Precision

Computerized panel sizing with laser-guided edge trimming and 32mm system line-boring with micro-metric 0.1mm tolerance.

  • 3D Laser room survey integration

From a Boutique Workshop to Industry-Leading Cabinet Makers

Founded in 2011 by master woodcraft engineer Henrik Vance, MODULARIS began with a clear mission: to eliminate mass-produced particle board furniture and replace it with precision German-engineered modular wardrobe architecture.

Today, our 40,000 sq. ft. automated manufacturing facility combines 5-axis CNC router accuracy with traditional hand-rubbed timber finishes, delivering custom walk-in wardrobes and kitchen cabinetry to over 4,800 luxury homes.

Who We Are

Pioneering Modular Joinery & Cabinet Engineering

The Company: Founded in 2011 by master joinery engineer Henrik Vance, MODULARIS is an industry-leading architectural manufacturer specializing in bespoke modular wardrobes and premium custom cabinetry. What began as an artisan workshop in Munich has scaled into a state-of-the-art 40,000 sq. ft. automated manufacturing facility dedicated to eliminating the delays, noise, and airborne sawdust of traditional on-site carpentry.

Our Work

We design and fabricate custom modular wardrobes (hinged, sliding, walk-in, L-shaped alcoves), waterproof modular kitchens, floating media consoles, and luxury vanities.

Our Experience

15+ years of engineering mastery combining European craftsmanship with 5-axis German Homag CNC machinery, 0.1mm tolerances, and zero-joint PUR edge-banding.

Polyurethane reactive hot-melt adhesive bonds 1.3mm–2mm PVC/ABS bands creating an imperceptible, moisture-impermeable seal on all edges.

  • Invisible zero glue line technology

Production Excellence

Our 4-Stage Modular Manufacturing Lifecycle

How automated factory joinery ensures flawless fit and 48-hour dust-free installation.

01

3D Laser CAD Survey

Laser-scanned room dimensions accurate to 0.5mm are converted to parametric CAD nest blueprints.

02

5-Axis CNC Milling

German CNC machines route panels to 0.1mm tolerance and precision drill 32mm system hardware holes.

03

PUR Zero-Joint Edging

Polyurethane hot-melt adhesive creates a seamless zero-joint edge that seals 100% against humidity.

04

48h Dust-Free Assembly

Pre-finished modular panels arrive barcoded in crates and assemble in 1-2 days with zero home sawdust.

Pricing & Payment FAQs

Our per-foot price includes laser room scanning, complete 3D VR CAD modeling, factory CNC milling, soft-close hardware, delivery, and professional white-glove on-site installation.

We operate on a 40% initial deposit upon 3D CAD design approval, 50% prior to factory shipment, and the remaining 10% upon final installation inspection and white-glove sign-off.

Standard production lead time is 6–10 weeks from design approval. Complex bespoke commissions (e.g. full walk-in suites with island units) may require 12–14 weeks. Rush production slots are available upon request.

The 4 Key Factors That Determine Your Price

Understanding how engineering choices, material substrates, and hardware mechanisms shape your final investment quote.

1. Core Board Substrate

CARB-2 Melamine offers the most affordable foundation. High-Density High Moisture Resistant (HDHMR) adds water resistance, while Boiling Water Proof (BWP) Marine Grade 710 ply is essential for sink bases and bathrooms.

2. Exterior Surface Finish

Matte factory laminates represent the base tier. Anti-fingerprint acrylic, clean-room Italian PU lacquer, natural smoked oak veneers, and fluted tempered bronze glass represent luxury decorative investments.

3. Hardware & Mechanisms

Standard soft-close hinges provide smooth operation. Austrian Blum Tandembox drawer runners, Hettich TopLine XL sliding systems, Aventos bi-fold lifts, and electric Servo-Drive mechanisms increase durability and convenience.

4. Architectural Complexity

Straight rectangular modules require standard machining. Sloped attic ceilings, 3-meter floor-to-ceiling lofts, intricate CNC jali cutwork, and custom corner carousels require specialized parametric CAD scribing.

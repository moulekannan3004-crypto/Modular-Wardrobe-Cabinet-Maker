How Our Modular Manufacturing Works

Why factory modular joinery delivers superior precision, flawless zero-joint edge banding, and rapid 48-hour on-site assembly.

01

3D CAD & Laser Audit

Laser-scanned room measurements accurate to 0.5mm are converted into parametric 3D fabrication blueprints with automated nest optimization.

02

5-Axis CNC Precision

Automated German CNC machinery routes panels to 0.1mm tolerance and precision-bores all 32mm system minifix, dowel, and hinge holes.

03

Zero-Joint PUR Edging

Industrial 2mm polymer edge bands applied with polyurethane (PUR) hot-melt adhesive create an invisible joint that seals 100% against humidity.

04

48h Dust-Free Install

Pre-finished modular panels are delivered barcoded in protective crating and assembled cleanly in your home in 1-2 days with zero sawdust.

Manufacturing, Design & Quality

Technical Excellence in Joinery & Hardware

From 5-axis German CNC milling and zero-joint industrial PUR edge-banding to Austrian Blum hardware and CARB 2 certified substrates, every modular unit is engineered for lifetime structural performance.

MANUFACTURING

5-Axis CNC Precision & System 32 Boring

Automated German Homag CNC machinery routes panels to 0.1mm tolerance. Precision multi-spindle drilling creates 32mm system shelf pin holes, minifix cam housings, and hinge mortises with zero manual error.

Tolerance: < 0.1 mm
QUALITY

Industrial Zero-Joint PUR Edge Sealing

Polyurethane (PUR) reactive hot-melt adhesive creates an impermeable moisture seal with an invisible zero glue line. Resists up to 150°C heat, water immersion, and prevents edge delamination.

100% Moisture Proof Seal
DESIGN

18mm HDHMR & BWP Marine Substrates

High-Density High Moisture Resistance cores (850+ kg/m³ density) offering exceptional 2,200N screw-holding capacity. Wet zones use 100% Boiling Water Proof (BWP) Marine Ply with zero formaldehyde emissions.

CARB 2 & E0 Certified
HARDWARE

Blum & Hettich European Mechanics

Full-extension Blum Movento undermount runners and Hettich TopLine XL top-hung sliding hardware tested to 200,000 cycles. Adaptive fluid BLUMOTION dampers eliminate door slamming and sagging.

Tested to 200,000 Cycles
